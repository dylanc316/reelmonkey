Removing intimate images shared without consent

If a reel on ReelMonkey shows you (or someone you're authorized to act for) in an intimate image or video that was shared without consent, including images that were AI-generated or edited to look like you, you can ask us to remove it using this form.

  • Your request needs: your name and email, enough detail for us to find the content, a statement that it was shared without consent, and your typed signature.
  • We remove valid requests within 48 hours of receiving them, and we also remove copies of the same content that we find on ReelMonkey.
  • You don't need a ReelMonkey account to make a request.

You can also use this form for other problems, such as copyright, spam or harassment.
