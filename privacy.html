Privacy policy

Effective October 22, 2026

ReelMonkey ("we", "us") is a play-money platform for investing in short-form videos. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. ReelMonkey is not affiliated with Instagram or Meta.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address, username and password. Passwords are stored only in hashed form by our authentication provider.
  • Date of birth: we ask for it when you sign up to confirm you're old enough to use ReelMonkey. We check it and then discard it; we keep only a record that you met the age requirement.
  • Content you upload: your videos, captions and profile picture.
  • Activity on ReelMonkey: your likes and dislikes, play-money trades, positions, balance and daily bonuses.
  • Reports: if you submit a report or removal request, the details you provide, such as your name, email, signature and description.
  • Technical information: our service providers automatically receive information such as your IP address and browser details when you use the site, and keep it in logs. For reports, we keep a scrambled (hashed) form of your network address only to limit spam.

How we use it

  • To run your account, show your reels and profile, and process your play-money trades.
  • To keep ReelMonkey safe: preventing abuse, reviewing reports, removing content that breaks our rules or the law, and complying with legal obligations.
  • To contact you about your account or a report you submitted.

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't use it for advertising.

What other people can see

  • Signed-in users can see your username, profile picture, reels, captions and the market activity shown on reels (such as like counts).
  • The leaderboard shows the usernames, profile pictures and play-money profits of top traders.
  • Your videos and profile picture are served from public links, so anyone who has a link can view that file.
  • Your email address, trades, positions and balance are not shown to other users.

Service providers we use

We share information with these companies only so they can run parts of ReelMonkey for us:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in, profile picture storage and server functions.
  • Mux: video upload, processing and streaming.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr: deliver the fonts and code libraries the site loads, which means your browser contacts their servers.

We may also disclose information when required by law, for example to respond to valid legal process, or to report child sexual exploitation to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children as federal law requires.

How long we keep it

  • Your account information, content and activity are kept until you delete them or your account.
  • Reports and records of moderation actions are kept as long as needed to show how we handled them.
  • Content we're legally required to preserve, such as material reported to NCMEC, is kept for the period the law requires (currently one year) and then deleted.

Your choices

  • You can delete your reels at any time, and change your profile picture from your profile page.
  • You can permanently delete your account, reels, shares and profile picture using "Delete account" at the bottom of the site.
  • To ask for a copy of your information or for help deleting it, contact us at the address below.

Age requirement

ReelMonkey is only for people 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

We use reputable providers and access controls to protect your information, but no online service can guarantee complete security.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we'll let you know on the site.

Contact

Questions or requests: reelmonkeysupport@gmail.com. To report content or request removal of an intimate image, use our report page.
